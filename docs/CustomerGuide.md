# Customer Guide

## Getting started

1. Receive invitation email (or owner credentials from your administrator).
2. Accept invitation / set password.
3. Sign in at the Customer Portal URL provided by TechGuard.

## What you can do

- View organisation dashboard and licences
- Download active TG1 licence files (Technical / Admin / Owner)
- Manage users and invitations (Owner / Admin)
- View billing: subscription, plan, invoices, payments (Owner / Admin / Finance)
- Start Stripe Checkout for eligible plans (Owner / Admin / Finance) when online payments are enabled
- Renew, upgrade, schedule downgrade, cancel at period end, and undo cancellation from Billing
- Update payment method via Stripe-hosted portal (payment method only)
- Manage billing email / invoice recipients in Billing settings
- Compare plans (price, devices, features, support)
- Manage sessions and profile

## What you cannot do yet

- Self-serve upgrade/downgrade — **available in Phase 5B** (upgrade via Checkout; downgrade scheduled to next period)
- Custom card entry forms (Checkout / Billing Portal are Stripe-hosted only)
- MFA / SSO

## Tips

- Use **Remember me** only on trusted devices.
- Revoke unknown sessions under **Sessions**.
- Contact your organisation Owner for role changes.
