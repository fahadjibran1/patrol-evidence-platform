## 3. Processor obligations to draft fully

The signed terms must address:

- processing only on documented instructions, including transfers, unless law requires otherwise;
- confidentiality commitments for authorised personnel;
- appropriate technical and organisational security measures in an approved annex;
- subprocessor approval, list, change notice and equivalent obligations;
- assistance with rights requests, security, breach notification, DPIAs and regulator consultation;
- deletion or return at end of service, including copies, unless retention is legally required;
- information and audits needed to demonstrate compliance;
- immediate notice if an instruction appears unlawful;
- scope, costs and limits of assistance only where legally permissible and fair;
- allocation of controller and processor incident/notification duties;
- international-transfer mechanisms and transfer risk assessments.

## 4. Proposed support controls for review

- Data minimisation: diagnostics before database; database before evidence; never request LocalAuth unless specifically justified.
- Customer authorisation and case reference before access.
- Approved encrypted transfer and storage only; no public links or ordinary email attachments for sensitive data.
- Named, least-privilege support personnel with access logging and confidentiality duties.
- Separate case workspace; no production/development reuse or training use.
- Malware scanning and integrity checks appropriate to submitted files.
- Defined deletion deadline and customer confirmation.
