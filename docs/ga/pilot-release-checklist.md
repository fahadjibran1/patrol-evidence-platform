- [ ] Customer receives the Quick Start.
- [ ] Customer receives or can access the Privacy Notice.
- [ ] Customer receives and accepts the applicable Software Licence Terms.
- [ ] Customer receives Support details.

## Controlled delivery

- [ ] Package was placed in a restricted Vesoft-managed OneDrive/SharePoint location or delivered through an individually controlled mechanism.
- [ ] Recipient access was restricted to the approved business contact(s).
- [ ] No unrestricted public or anonymous sharing link was used.
- [ ] Delivered package hash was verified after upload or transfer.
- [ ] Delivery date and package hash were recorded in the pilot record.
