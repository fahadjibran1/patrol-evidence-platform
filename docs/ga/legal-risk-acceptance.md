Decision authority: **Vesoft Services Limited / company director**

## Decision and scope

Vesoft Services Limited management has elected to proceed with the current frozen PatrolSafe v1.0.0 legal-document set without external legal counsel review. This is a business-risk acceptance for the following controlled B2B pilot scope only:

- Stage 1: Vesoft/internal controlled deployment.
- Stage 2: one to three friendly business pilot customers.

Each pilot customer and its jurisdiction must be recorded and approved by Vesoft before supply. An approved pilot customer may be international. This decision does not authorise unrestricted worldwide public GA, consumer marketing, an unrestricted public download, or automatic expansion to additional jurisdictions. PatrolSafe does not implement a permanent country allowlist or geoblocking.

## Risks not externally reviewed

Management acknowledges that external legal advice has not been obtained for:

- the Privacy Notice and privacy-law interpretation;
- customer/Vesoft controller and processor roles;
- DPA applicability and terms;
- international transfers;
- sanctions and export restrictions;
- EULA enforceability;
- governing law and jurisdiction;
- liability and warranty limitations;
- refund and cancellation provisions;
- evidence, support-record and licensing-record retention/deletion obligations;
- breach and incident responsibilities;
- regional consumer and business law;
- LGPL and copyleft obligations; and
- special or compound third-party licence expressions.

These risks are accepted for the limited controlled B2B pilot; they are not treated as legally resolved.

LGPL and special/compound licence interpretations are **MANAGEMENT RISK ACCEPTED FOR CONTROLLED PILOT** and **EXTERNAL LEGAL REVIEW NOT PERFORMED**. This is not a finding of legal compliance.

## Operational controls

- Privacy enquiries route to the operator-confirmed `privacy@sfour.co.uk` mailbox.
- Sensitive support files use a Vesoft-controlled Microsoft OneDrive/SharePoint restricted transfer workflow; unrestricted public links are prohibited.
- Manual licence recovery normally targets one to two business days during support hours. This is not an SLA or guarantee.
- Normal support follows the approved data-minimisation escalation order.
- Vesoft/company director remains the release authority and must separately authorise each distribution action.

## Future review

External legal review is recommended before Stage 3 controlled public availability and is strongly recommended before broader international scaling. A future reviewer must work from the exact hashed snapshot and record any approved revisions by filename and hash.
