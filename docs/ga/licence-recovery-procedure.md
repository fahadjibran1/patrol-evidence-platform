# PatrolSafe v1.0.0 licence recovery procedure

Status: **MANAGEMENT APPROVED FOR PATROLSAFE v1.0 CONTROLLED PILOT — EXTERNAL LEGAL REVIEW NOT PERFORMED**

Moving PatrolSafe to another workstation may require licence recovery. Contact **support@sfour.co.uk** and provide your Installation ID.

## Customer process

1. The customer contacts `support@sfour.co.uk` from an authorised business contact.
2. The customer supplies the company name, current or replacement Installation ID, licence/customer reference where available, and the reason for replacement or recovery.
3. Vesoft Services Limited verifies the request and the requester's authority using proportionate account, order and contact records. Possession of an Installation ID alone is not proof of authority.
4. The old or replaced installation is identified and retired where applicable. Vesoft records any exception rather than silently creating an additional device entitlement.
5. An authorised Vesoft operator generates or issues the replacement licence using the controlled licence process.
6. The customer imports/activates the licence on the replacement workstation and confirms the displayed company, term and installation identity.
7. Vesoft records the request, verification, old and new installation references, decision, issuer, date and outcome under the approved support/licensing retention schedule.

This is a manual v1 process. PatrolSafe does not offer automatic entitlement transfer, automatic renewal or self-service workstation reassignment.

Licence recovery requests are normally processed within **1–2 business days** during PatrolSafe support hours. This is an operational target, not an SLA or guaranteed response or completion time. Verification questions or suspected misuse may require longer.

## Safety and fraud controls

- Escalate mismatched company, contact, order, licence or installation details; do not issue while authority is uncertain.
- Use only the minimum identity and licence information necessary for verification.
- Never request a customer's password, WhatsApp profile, private signing key or Vesoft licence-signing secret.
- Do not send licence-signing material or internal signing instructions to a customer.
- Use the approved secure support-transfer route if sensitive evidence is exceptionally required; ordinary email attachments are not the default.
- Record suspected fraud or account compromise through the security route at `security@sfour.co.uk`.

## Operating ownership

Licence-recovery authority is **Vesoft Services Limited**. Release/signing authority remains with **Vesoft Services Limited / company director**. Internal access controls and retention periods remain controlled operating matters; the approved normal turnaround target is 1–2 business days during support hours and is not an SLA.
