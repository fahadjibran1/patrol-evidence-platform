## Technical transmission conclusion

No automatic Vesoft telemetry, evidence upload or cloud backup client was found. Vesoft receives licensing or support data only through a deliberate customer action in the audited v1 design. WhatsApp/Meta and Microsoft operate independent service/runtime data flows. Counsel must confirm controller/processor roles, support terms, retention, international transfers and customer deployment duties before publication.
