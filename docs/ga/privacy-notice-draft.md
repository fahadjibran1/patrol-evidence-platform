## 2. How the installed product works

PatrolSafe is a local-first Windows desktop application. Company details, local users, sites, mappings, patrol images, evidence metadata, operational records, workspace configuration and logs are normally stored on the customer's workstation or customer-selected storage. The application does not automatically upload that operational database or evidence to Vesoft, and no Vesoft telemetry or cloud-backup client was identified in the audited v1.0 build.

PatrolSafe connects an authorised WhatsApp account through WhatsApp Web. WhatsApp/Meta processes communications and account/source information under its own terms and privacy materials. Windows, Microsoft Edge, SmartScreen, certificate validation and update services may communicate with Microsoft independently.

The workspace stores one IANA time zone selected by the customer. Event timestamps remain canonical instants; PatrolSafe uses the saved zone to display and group evidence and interpret patrol schedules. Changing it can change displayed dates and schedule interpretation but does not alter the original event instant, evidence file, integrity hash or WhatsApp message identity.

## 5. Customer operational data

The customer determines why and how PatrolSafe monitors its sites and personnel, which groups are mapped, who has access, how long evidence is retained and who receives it. The customer is normally expected to act as controller or the corresponding responsible party for that operational processing.

If Vesoft receives or accesses operational data solely to support the customer, Vesoft may act as processor/service provider. The parties must establish the applicable roles and written terms before access. Vesoft will not request a database, backup, evidence folder or WhatsApp session when narrower diagnostics are sufficient.

The customer must select a workspace time zone appropriate to its operations and explain time-based monitoring where relevant. All sites in a v1 workspace use that one zone.

## 9. Security

PatrolSafe uses local access controls, salted password hashes, integrity hashes, signed software and verified backup/restore mechanisms. Customer evidence and SQLite data are not encrypted by PatrolSafe at rest. Customers should use supported Windows security, device/storage encryption, least privilege, protected backups and physical security.

No internet service can be guaranteed completely secure. Product vulnerability reports may be sent to **security@sfour.co.uk**. Incident-notification duties, process and timing have not received external legal review; management accepted that unresolved risk for controlled pilot use.
